## Document policy

The key words `MUST`, `MUST NOT`, `REQUIRED`, `SHALL`, `SHALL NOT`, `SHOULD`,
`SHOULD NOT`, `RECOMMENDED`, `NOT RECOMMENDED`, `MAY`, and `OPTIONAL` in
Standards Track documents are interpreted as described by BCP 14 when, and only
when, they appear in all capitals.

Protocol documents describe wire behavior independently of a particular host
language, NFC controller, microcontroller, operating system, or game. Product
and implementation details belong under [`impl/`](../impl/README.md).
